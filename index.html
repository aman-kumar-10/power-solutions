    Technical Guidance

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Essential answers on electrical licensing, HT/LT compliance, panel design, and government certifications.

    A Class "A" license is the highest electrical contractor certification authorized by the Chief Electrical Inspector to Government (CEIG), Punjab. It qualifies Power Solutions to execute extra-high-voltage (EHV), High Tension (HT above 1kV up to 33kV), and Low Tension (LT) electrical installations, power substations, transformers, and industrial distribution systems throughout Punjab and across India.

    Power Solutions is an authorized dealer and certified partner of world-class electrical brands including ABB, Schneider Electric, Havells, L&T Electrical, Siemens, Polycab, KEI Wires, RR Kabel, Finolex, V-Guard, C&S Electric, Anchor, and Legrand. All supplied materials come with factory test certificates and OEM warranties.

    We custom-fabricate Power Control Centers (PCC), Motor Control Centers (MCC), Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) panels, Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) panels, DG synchronization panels, and VFD control cabinets. All enclosures feature Form-4 segregation, modular sheet steel, and up to IP55 protection ratings.

    Yes. Our team handles complete statutory CEIG liaisoning, drafting single line diagrams (SLD), conducting earth-resistance and dielectric breakdown testing, submitting documentation, and escorting government inspectors for swift plant energization clearances.

    Our central facility is at Office no 12A plot no E 45 phase 8 industrial area Mohali, Punjab. We regularly execute turnkey projects across Mohali, Kharar, Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Patiala, and throughout North India.

    Power Solutions is officially certified by the Government of Punjab as a Class "A" Electrical Contractor, registered under the Ministry of MSME, Government of India with Udyam Registration number UDYAM-PB-20-0103957, and registered for GST under GSTIN 03BFVPT8691B1ZB.
